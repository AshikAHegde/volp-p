HOW THIS APP WORKS (SIMPLE GUIDE)
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW — IN SIMPLE, EVERYDAY WORDS
STUDENT TIME-SAVER

Never Miss Homework or an Assignment Again

Logging into the college website every day and clicking into 16 separate subjects just to check if something is due takes forever. This tool does the boring work for you: it gathers all your tasks into one clean screen and sends you an instant Telegram alert every evening at 8:00 PM with what's due!

01 Safe 1-Time Login
02 1-Click Auto-Fetch
03 Track Deadlines
04 Mute Old Subjects
05 Daily 8 PM Telegram
STEP 01 100% PRIVATE & SAFE

1. Safe & Simple Sign-In

Log in using your existing college portal username and password. You only have to log in once! The app connects directly with the official university portal and securely remembers you so you stay signed in.

👤 You Sign In Once Normal College Login
➔
🏫 College Portal Checks Your Account
➔
🔒 Safe & Remembered Zero Password Storage
  • Your password is never saved anywhere — complete security and privacy.
  • You don't need to re-enter your credentials every time you open the app.
  • Works smoothly even if the college portal is temporarily slow.
STEP 02 SAVES 20-30 MIN DAILY

2. Gathers All Subjects in 1 Click

Normally, you must click through every subject, unit, and chapter to check for new work. Clicking SYNC VOLP automatically checks all your 16+ courses in the background and brings every assignment into one unified list.

🔄 One Button Checks All 16+ Subjects
THEORY HOMEWORK Written Questions Prompts, theory answers & questions
LAB PRACTICALS Projects & Lab Files Code uploads, slide decks & presentations
  • Collects both written assignments and lab/practical submissions.
  • Knows automatically if you already submitted your answer or if it's pending.
  • Shows the exact deadline and marks for every single task.
STEP 03 EASY COLOR CODING

3. Track Deadlines at a Glance

No more writing deadlines on scraps of paper. See everything laid out neatly with clear color labels: pending homework, what's due soon, and what's already completed. You can also view them on a monthly calendar!

Operating Systems NEEDS ACTION
Unit 3: Process Scheduling Analysis & Report
📅 Due in 2 Days (Thursday) ⚖️ 10 Marks
  • Green tags for work you already finished; yellow/red for work coming up.
  • Filter by any subject or search by keyword in one second.
  • Click the Calendar tab to see your entire month scheduled like a personal planner.
STEP 04 NO CLUTTER

4. Silence Finished or Extra Subjects

Enrolled in an elective or lab that's already over, or a subject you don't need reminders for? Just click "Block" to mute it. It stays safely on your college portal, but stops cluttering your screen and inbox.

🔇 SUBJECT MUTED
➔
✓ Removed from your daily view ✓ Silenced from evening Telegram alerts
  • Mute whole subjects or individual tasks with one click.
  • Keeps your daily view focused only on subjects that actually matter right now.
  • You can un-mute any subject at any time under the "BLOCKED ITEMS" tab.
STEP 05 AUTOMATIC EVENING REMINDER

5. Daily 8:00 PM Telegram Alert Directly to Your Phone

You don't even have to open this website every day! Every evening at 8:00 PM, the system automatically checks your pending homework across all active subjects and sends your Telegram bot a clean, instant push notification with only the tasks you still need to finish.
