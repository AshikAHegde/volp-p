ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, scraping, and notifications.

Does this share my university login password with anyone? ▼
No. Your authentication is isolated to your local instance and session. When you log in, session tokens are securely handled and passwords are never exposed or transmitted to external third parties.
Will using this tool cause my university portal account to be banned? ▼
No. VOLP Reminder includes a dedicated Strict Rate Limiter (capped at 10 sync calls per 15 minutes) and uses MongoDB caching. Browsing your assignments reads directly from the fast local database cache, eliminating excessive portal requests.
What time does the automated email reminder arrive? ▼
The built-in cron scheduler triggers every evening at 8:00 PM. It filters for assignments due tonight or tomorrow and dispatches an HTML email. You can also manually trigger a reminder anytime from the dashboard.
Can I hide or mute courses that I have already finished? ▼
Yes! You can mute entire courses or individual homework items from the "Courses" or "Assignments" tab. Muted items are excluded from your pending counts and will not trigger 8:00 PM email notifications.
