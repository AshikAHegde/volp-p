COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Does this save or share my university password? ▼
No. Your password is only used once to log in to the university portal and obtain a session token. The password itself is never stored in the database or shared anywhere. Only the session token is saved locally to keep you signed in.
Will this cause problems with my university portal account? ▼
No. The tool syncs your data carefully and uses a local database cache so it doesn't hammer the university server. Once your assignments are fetched, the dashboard reads from the local cache instantly — the portal is only contacted when you manually sync.
What time does the Telegram reminder arrive? ▼
The system automatically sends a reminder alert every evening at 8:00 PM directly to your Telegram bot. It lists all assignments due tonight and tomorrow. You can also send yourself a reminder manually anytime from the Reminders section of the dashboard.
Can I hide courses I've already finished? ▼
Yes. From the Courses or Assignments tab, you can mute any course or individual assignment with one click. Muted items are removed from your pending counts, dashboard views, and will not trigger Telegram notifications.
My assignments aren't showing up — what should I do? ▼
Click the "Sync VOLP" button in the dashboard to fetch the latest data from the university portal. If you just logged in for the first time, the system fetches your data automatically. Make sure you're connected to the internet and your portal session is active.
